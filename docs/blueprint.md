# **App Name**: DigiPajak UMKM

## Core Features:

- Dashboard: Display aggregated annual turnover and tax compliance status, along with important notifications.
- Tax Simulation: Simulate tax obligations based on aggregated annual turnover, calculating PPh Final using the defined formula and providing a simulation report.
- Tax-Free Threshold Tracker: Visualize progress towards the tax-free threshold with a dynamic progress bar and clear indicators, displaying turnover data and tax implications.
- E-Commerce Platform Integration: Automatically connect and sync turnover data from various e-commerce platforms via API, displaying connection statuses, logs, and turnover contributions.
- Incentive Module: Display potential tax incentives and simulate tax billing codes to help UMKM optimize their tax strategies.
- Education and Regulation Module: Provide interactive information on the latest regulations, FAQs, and case studies to educate UMKM on tax compliance.
- User Authentication: Enable user login/registration with data storage (user_id, business name, NPWP, total turnover, platform turnover, integration status, simulated PPh final), using JWT or session cookies for authentication.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Dark blue (#1E3A8A) to convey trust and stability, reminiscent of government digital services.
- Background color: Light blue (#D8E6F2), a desaturated version of the primary color, for a clean and professional look.
- Accent color: Soft green (#A3E4D7), analogous to blue, for positive feedback and progress indicators.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a sans-serif font, provides a modern, neutral, and readable appearance suitable for dashboard elements.
- Use lucide-react or feather icons for a consistent, clean, and professional visual language.
- Responsive layout adaptable to desktop, tablet, and mobile devices, ensuring accessibility and usability across platforms.
- Employ smooth transitions and animations for progress bars, numerical displays, and charts to enhance user engagement and provide clear feedback.
